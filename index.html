Resolute

How can we help?

Resolute helps you plan your year, turn your goals into daily habits, and see every week whether you're on track.

Contact us
Write to resoluteplannerapp@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Subscriptions

Resolute is purchased through the App Store. To cancel or change a subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge.

Bought Resolute on another iPhone or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings inside the app.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your goals, habits and personal reasons stay on your iPhone. In the app, Settings → Your data lets you export everything as a file or delete it all.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Sign in with Apple is optional.

Does my accountability partner need the app? No. You send them a short weekly check-in from Messages, WhatsApp or any app you like.

Reminders don't arrive. Check that notifications are on for Resolute in your iPhone's Settings, and in the app under Settings → Reminders.
