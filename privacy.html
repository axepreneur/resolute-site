Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The short version: your goals, habits, notes and personal reasons stay on your iPhone. We use anonymous-style analytics to learn which screens work, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

Everything you write or plan in Resolute (goals, your "why", habits, routines, tasks, reviews, chapters, checkpoints and your accountability partner's name) is stored only on your iPhone. We don't have a server that receives it.

If you use Sign in with Apple, the Apple user ID, name and email Apple shares with the app are also stored only on your iPhone.

What we collect

We use PostHog to understand how people use the app. It receives:

  • Product interaction: which screens you view and which actions you take (for example "goal set" or "review completed"), with counts and categories such as how many goals you set or which life area your main goal belongs to. Never the text you write.
  • Purchase history: which plan you bought and whether it started with a free trial.
  • Identifiers: a random app identifier and, if you use Sign in with Apple, your Apple user ID, so your usage counts as one person across reinstalls.
  • Technical data: app version, iOS version, device model and approximate country, sent automatically with each event.

We use this data only for analytics and to improve Resolute. We don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use it for advertising. We don't record your screen.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. When you send a check-in to your accountability partner, you send it yourself through the app you choose (Messages, WhatsApp, etc.), and only what you see in the preview is shared. Your personal reasons are never included.

Your choices

  • Export or delete everything stored on your device from Settings → Your data in the app. Deleting the app also deletes it.
  • To ask us to delete the analytics data linked to your device or Apple user ID, write to resoluteplannerapp@gmail.com.

Children

Resolute is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
