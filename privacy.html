Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

The short version: when you plan in the app, your goals, habits, notes and personal reasons stay on your iPhone. If you make your plan on our website instead, that plan is saved with your account so the app can open it. Your account (email, name and a user ID) is stored with Google Firebase so you can sign in. We use analytics to learn which screens work, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

What stays on your device

This section is about plans made in the iPhone app. Plans made on the website are covered in the next section.

Everything you write or plan in Resolute (goals, your "why", habits, routines, tasks, reviews, chapters, checkpoints and your accountability partner's name) is stored only on your iPhone. It is never sent to our account system, to analytics or to anyone else.

Plans made on the website

You can also build your 2027 plan on our website (for example from a link we send you on Instagram). Because the website can't hand the plan to your iPhone directly, it is saved with your account in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Google Firebase, servers in the United States): your goals, the reasons you wrote, your routine, your year plan, the habits you want to stop and your accountability partner's first name, if you added one. Only you can read it: it's tied to your account and protected so no other account can access it. When you sign in to the iPhone app with the same account, the app downloads it and from then on it works like any plan made in the app.

While you're on the website, your progress is also kept in your browser so a refresh doesn't lose it. If you ask us to email you your plan, we send it only to the address on your account.

Deleting your account (see "Your choices") deletes the saved plan too.

Your account

To save your progress you create an account with Sign in with Apple, Google, or an email and password. The account is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores:

  • Contact info: your email address, and your name and profile photo link if Apple or Google share them. If you use "Hide My Email" with Apple, we only get the relay address.
  • Identifiers: a user ID for your account and how you signed in.
  • Security data: sign-in dates and the technical data Firebase needs to keep accounts safe, such as IP address.

We use this only to create your account and let you sign in. Firebase stores it on Google's servers in the United States.

Purchases

In the app, payments are processed by Apple. On the website, payments are processed by our payment providers (RevenueCat Web Billing and Stripe). We never see your full card details.

We use RevenueCat to process and verify purchases and subscriptions. It receives your account user ID, your purchase and subscription history (which plan, when it started, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds), which paywall you saw, and technical data such as device model, iOS version and country.

Analytics

We use PostHog to understand how people use the app. It receives:

  • Product interaction: which screens you view and which actions you take (for example "goal set" or "review completed"), with counts and categories such as how many goals you set or which life area your main goal belongs to. Never the text you write.
  • Purchase history: which plan you bought, whether it started with a free trial, and later subscription events (trial converted, renewed, cancelled) sent by RevenueCat.
  • Identifiers: a random app identifier and your account user ID, so your usage counts as one person across reinstalls. Never your email or name.
  • Technical data: app version, iOS version, device model and approximate country, sent automatically with each event.

We use this data only for analytics and to improve Resolute. We don't use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use it for advertising. We don't record your screen.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. The services above (Google Firebase, RevenueCat and PostHog) process it only to provide their service to us. When you send a check-in to your accountability partner, you send it yourself through the app you choose (Messages, WhatsApp, etc.), and only what you see in the preview is shared. Your personal reasons are never included.

Your choices

  • Export everything stored on your device from Settings → Your data in the app.
  • Delete your account and everything on your device from Settings → Your data → Delete my account and data. You'll confirm it's you, then your account and any plan you made on the website are deleted from Firebase and, if you used Sign in with Apple, the app's access to your Apple ID is revoked. Deleting the app alone doesn't delete your account.
  • To ask us to delete the analytics and purchase records linked to your account, write to resoluteplannerapp@gmail.com.
  • Your subscription is managed by Apple: cancel it from your Apple ID settings. If you subscribed on the website, cancel it from the link in your receipt email or by writing to us.
  • Emails: if you started on the website, we may email you your plan and occasional tips. Every email has a one-tap unsubscribe link. In the EU, UK and Switzerland we only send tips if you ticked the box to receive them.

Children

Resolute is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
